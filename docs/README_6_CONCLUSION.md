# Conclusion

I was not initially interested in drawing a broad conclusion. Because this project took so long, I feel that maybe a conclusion is in order.

Companies like OpenAI are constantly working on new Large Language Models. They are searching for a level of intelligence that is higher than a human being. This is termed as AGI - Advanced General Intelligence. They claim they are close to this goal. If they were close, then the simple task of playing a game like Pong would be trivial. In fact the most recent models, though they can play, do not play masterfully. They should be able to win in every case, but in fact they cannot. In order to get them to win all the time, special measures must be taken that are very technical and specific. When OpenAI finally produces AGI, you should be able to start up any video game and the model should play well. The conclusion here is that they have not reached AGI.

These models do other things very well, but they cannot be AGI if they cannot do everything very well.

It is not clear to me whether or not OpenAI trains their models with Atari 2600 games. The so called AGI model, regardless, would be able to win despite it's specific training.
